# Prospect Qualification Checklist

Use this **before** adding a business to `tracker.csv`. Takes about 10
minutes per prospect. Full criteria: `docs/EXPERIMENT-001.md` §4.

**A prospect qualifies only when at least 2 of the 6 checks below are
objectively true, with evidence you can point to.** Never check a box you
can't back up. If you have to stretch to find a defect, the prospect is
not qualified — drop it and move to the next one.

## The six checks

Search: `"[service] in [area]"` exactly as a customer would type it.
Screenshot or note the result.

- [ ] **1. Poor ranking.** Not on page 1, or below 3 competitors, for the
  exact search above.
- [ ] **2. Website failure.** No website, or one that visibly breaks on
  your phone — doesn't load, menu unusable, no clear call to action.
- [ ] **3. Incomplete Google Business Profile.** Missing hours, no
  photos, or fewer than ~10 reviews.
- [ ] **4. Broken contact path.** Try it. Contact form doesn't submit,
  phone number is wrong or unanswered, listed email bounces or is clearly
  unmonitored.
- [ ] **5. Inconsistent information.** Compare Google Business, Justdial,
  and Instagram/Facebook. Different hours, address, or phone number
  across two or more.
- [ ] **6. Stale social activity.** Last post 3+ months old on a platform
  they otherwise use, while the business is visibly still operating
  (open, staffed, serving customers).

**Count checked boxes: ____ / 6**

- **0–1 checked → do not qualify.** No verifiable problem, or too weak to
  open a conversation honestly.
- **2+ checked → qualified.** Write the strongest one as your opening
  line. It's the only sentence of the pitch that has to work.

## Record before moving on

```
Business:              ____________________
Vertical:               ____________________
Checks passed (list):   ____________________
Strongest evidence:     ____________________
                         (this becomes the audit's headline finding)
Decision-maker:          owner / manager / unknown — name if known
```

Copy this into `prospects/tracker.csv` as a new row, then proceed to
`audit/audit-template.md`.
