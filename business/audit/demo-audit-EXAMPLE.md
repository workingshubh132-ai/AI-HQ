# Sunrise Dental Clinic — Online Presence Audit

*Prepared by [your name] · [date] · No charge, no obligation*

## What I checked

I searched `"dentist in Koramangala"` — the way a customer looking for a
dentist in your area actually would.

## What I found

**1. Search visibility**

Sunrise Dental Clinic appears at position 9 for that search. Two nearby
clinics — Smile Care Dental and Koramangala Dental Studio — appear in the
first three results.

*Why it matters:* most people call one of the first two or three results
and stop looking. Those searches are currently reaching your competitors
first.

**2. Google Business Profile**

Your listing has 4 reviews and no photos. Smile Care Dental, which
outranks you, has 87 reviews and a full photo set of the clinic interior.

*Why it matters:* a listing with almost no reviews or photos reads as
either new or inactive, even when it isn't — and Google's ranking weighs
this directly.

**3. Contact path**

The phone number listed on your Google Business Profile rang out with no
answer or voicemail across two attempts on a weekday afternoon.

*Why it matters:* a patient who can't reach you on the first try
typically calls the next name on the list, not yours a second time.

## What this is probably costing you

Harder to estimate precisely, but every week this stays as it is, the
top-of-search calls are going to the two clinics ranked ahead of you, not
to Sunrise.

## What I'd fix

- Add 10–15 real photos of the clinic and add a short "book now" call to
  action to the Google Business Profile
- Set up a way to know when the listed number goes unanswered, or route
  it somewhere that's reliably staffed
- Ask 15–20 recent patients for a review — this is usually the single
  biggest lever on local ranking
