# Offer Definitions

Source of truth for hypotheses: `docs/EXPERIMENT-001.md` §3. This file
exists so the exact scope of what's being sold is written down once,
rather than improvised differently each conversation.

## O1 — Digital front-door audit → fixed-price fix

**For:** H1 (local, owner-operated service businesses)

**The audit** (free, no obligation): a one-page document — see
`audit/audit-template.md` — identifying 1–3 specific, verified problems
in the business's online presence and what fixing them would involve.
This is the entire first touch. It is handed over before any money is
discussed.

**The fix** (paid, only if the audit leads to a yes):

| | Included | Not included |
|---|---|---|
| Standard scope | Google Business Profile cleanup (hours, photos, category, description) · fixing the specific contact-path defect found in the audit · basic on-page content correction if the site exists | A new website build from scratch · ongoing management · paid ads · content writing beyond correcting what's already there |

**Price anchor: ₹8,000.** Not proven — see the pricing rule below.

**Pricing rule (`EXPERIMENT-001.md` §9):** do not vary this price across
the first 20 prospects. If a prospect can't meet it, reduce *scope* to
fit — e.g. "₹5,000 for just the listing and contact-path fix" — and
record the reduced scope as its own line in the tracker, not as a
discount on the ₹8,000 offer. Never discount the same scope.

## O2 — Overflow web delivery

**For:** H2 (small web/marketing agencies, freelancers)

**The offer:** one small build or fix taken off an agency's plate for a
week, fixed price per job, delivered under their brand — they keep the
client relationship.

**Price:** negotiated per job. No anchor yet — H2 is a probe, not the
primary test, and price discovery for it is explicitly part of what this
round is for.

## What is never included in either offer

- No claim of results not yet observed (no "you'll get more customers,"
  only "you'll be easier to find" — see `EXPERIMENT-001.md` §14 on never
  fabricating customer feedback or outcomes)
- No fabricated case studies, testimonials, or past clients
- No commitment to a channel or tool not yet reviewed (e.g. don't promise
  "I'll also manage your Instagram" unless that's a decision already made)
