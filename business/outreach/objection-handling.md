# Objection Handling Framework

These are *directions*, not scripts — the real value of Experiment 001 is
finding out which objections actually appear and in what words. Record
every one verbatim in `prospects/tracker.csv`, even when it doesn't fit
this list.

## "We already have someone"

Often means a relative or a freelancer who built the site once and hasn't
touched it since.

> "When did they last update it? No worries if you're covered — happy to
> just leave the audit either way, in case it's useful later."

## "How much?"

A buying signal arriving earlier than expected. Never answer "it
depends."

> Give the fixed number from `offer/offer-definitions.md` directly.

## "We don't get customers online"

May be true — record it as a genuine pivot signal
(`docs/EXPERIMENT-001.md` §11), not something to argue past.

> "Where do most of your customers actually come from?" — then listen.
> This answer is often more valuable than the sale itself.

## "Send me the details / let me think about it"

Usually a polite decline, sometimes genuine.

> "I'll leave this with you — mind if I check back [specific day]?" —
> get a specific day, or record it as `TIMING` / `NOT_INTERESTED`
> honestly rather than treating a vague maybe as `INTERESTED`.

## Price objection

Record their reaction and, if offered, their own expected price
(`docs/EXPERIMENT-001.md` §9). Do not auto-discount — offer reduced scope
instead if you move at all, and log it as a separate line.

## After agreement, before payment

Verbal agreement is not `PAID`. Log as `NEGOTIATING` until money has
actually moved. If agreement is reached but payment doesn't follow within
a few days, that is itself a pivot signal worth recording
(`EXPERIMENT-001.md` §11) — it often means take payment up front, or
partially up front, on the next one.
